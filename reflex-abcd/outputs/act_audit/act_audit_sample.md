## What you are estimating

The **occurrence-weighted per-sentence accuracy** of `compile.act_labeler:
rules_plus_embed` over the **58,263 banked sentence occurrences** -- verified
2026-09-20 as the summed `count` of the 4,489 templates in
`outputs/compile/bank/templates.jsonl`, and identical to
`act_audit_key.json:total_bank_occurrences`. That is 65.01% of the 89,623 train
sentences: sentences whose only template fell below
`compile.min_template_count = 2` were never eligible to be sampled, so `c` is an
estimate over the BANK frame, not over train and not over the eval split.
`score_act_audit.py` prints that caveat in full; read it before attaching `c` to
any H5 number.

## The design, in numbers (from `act_audit_key.json`)

**120 occurrence draws** -- 30 per stratum, drawn i.i.d. with probability
proportional to template count -- which collapse to the **104 distinct
sentences** listed below; repeats are carried as a `draws` multiplicity in the
key, not deduped away. Stratum weights are occurrence shares of the 58,263 and
sum to 1.000:

| stratum | what it is | templates | occurrences | weight |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| A | rule:30 catch-all (sentence ends in `?` -> ASK) | 829 | 13,051 | 0.2240 |
| B | embedding tier (no cue rule matched) | 1,926 | 9,816 | 0.1685 |
| C | ASK / VERIFY line (identity-request and `\bverif` rules) | 272 | 6,378 | 0.1095 |
| D | other cue rules (ACK / CLOSE / OFFER / CONFIRM / INSTRUCT) | 1,462 | 29,018 | 0.4981 |

## How to do it (104 rows, ~17 s per row, ~30 min)

1. Read the sentence. Decide the act YOURSELF from the crib below, then look at
   the `label` column.
2. Put one character in `verdict`:  `y` = label is right, `n` = label is wrong,
   `?` = genuinely ambiguous (two acts defensible).
3. If `n`, put what it should be in `correct`. Leave `correct` blank otherwise.
4. Do NOT skip rows and do NOT go back and revise. Save the file.
5. Run: `python3 outputs/act_audit/score_act_audit.py`

**Some rows carry a deliberately corrupted label.** They are the acquiescence
control: if you rubber-stamp, the scorer will catch it and refuse to report an
accuracy (it needs at least 3 in 4 caught). They are excluded from the estimate
and carry `draws: 0`, so they do not enter any denominator. Do not try to find
them.

**When you read the scorer's output, note one mislabel in it.** The interval it
prints as `95% CI` is `[sum_s W_s*lo_s, sum_s W_s*hi_s]` — the weight-combination
of four independent per-stratum 95% Wilson intervals. That over-covers (joint
coverage roughly 0.95^4), so it is a **conservative envelope, not a 95% CI for
`c`**. Confirmed by running the scorer on a synthetic filled sheet on 2026-09-20:
with all four strata at 30/30 it prints `c = 1.000, 95% CI [0.886, 1.000]` — the
width of a *single* n=30 Wilson interval on a design with 120 draws. The Wald
version it replaced was genuinely broken (zero width at 30/30), so this is an
improvement; it is only the label that is wrong. Quote the envelope as an
envelope until `score_act_audit.py` is relabelled or switched to an interval
that actually targets 95%.

## Act crib (verbatim from `prompts/act_labeling.txt`)

| act | definition |
| --- | --- |
| ACK | acknowledging or thanking; social filler carrying no new information |
| VERIFY | checking a value the customer supplied, or asking them to confirm it |
| ASK | requesting information the agent does not yet have |
| INFORM | stating a fact, policy, price, status or result |
| INSTRUCT | telling the customer to do something |
| CONFIRM | stating that something the AGENT did is now done |
| OFFER | proposing an option or a remedy the customer may accept or decline |
| CLOSE | ending the conversation |
| OTHER | none of the above |

Two house conventions (`outputs/compile/act_check.md`) -- judge by these, not by taste:

- "is there anything else I can help you with?" is **OFFER**; "how can I help you?" is **ASK**.
- A bare greeting is **ACK**; a greeting welded to a question is scored **on the question**.

`{braces}` are delexicalized slot values. Judge the function, not the content.
